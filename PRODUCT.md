# EZAdd

What the product is, who it is for and what it must keep doing. `DESIGN.md` says how it looks
(written once a direction is picked from the lab, LAB-74), `MOTION.md` how it may move.

## Register

A small free tool, and a toy as much as a tool: it does one thing and should be a pleasure to
poke. Playful, relaxed and exact. It is an in-house OneLine product shown on oneline.software,
so the craft should show in how it answers the hand. It has its own look, not OneLine's and not
Family Funds': Family Funds is the calm one, EZAdd is the loud one.

## Users

- **Someone in a shop**, phone in one hand, adding up a basket before the till to see what it
  will cost, with the tax where prices are shown without it.
- **Someone at a table**, adding up a bill, a tip or a service charge, and splitting it.
- **Someone at a desk**, pasting a column of numbers from a spreadsheet to get a quick sum.
- **Portfolio visitors**, through `?demo`, which opens a filled basket and saves nothing.

## Purpose

Add prices up faster than a calculator app can: every price stays on the screen, can be fixed,
counted more than once, taxed and split, and the total is always in view.

## The one action

Type the next price. Everything else supports reading the total.

## Principles

1. **The total is always in view.** Above the keyboard on a phone, at the foot of the list
   everywhere else.
2. **Exact money.** Amounts are counted in whole cents (or fils, or yen) and written the way
   the currency is written. 12.50 stays 12.50.
3. **Nothing is lost.** The basket, the taxes and the settings are saved on the device after
   every change, and anything removed can be put back.
4. **Any currency, any place.** The currency is a setting, guessed once from the device.
   Tax presets cover countries, states and provinces, and every rate can be changed, because
   local rates differ.
5. **Works with no connection.** It installs to the home screen. Presets ship inside the app.
   Nothing about a basket leaves the device.
6. **Every press answers.** Keys go down, items drop in, the total changes where you can see
   it. Motion follows an action; it never decorates and never delays typing.
7. **Works without motion.** With reduced motion everything is still there and still clear.

## Out of scope for now

- Accounts, sync between devices, history of past baskets.
- Converting between currencies.
- Tax rules beyond a rate: exemptions, tax on tax, prices that already include tax.
